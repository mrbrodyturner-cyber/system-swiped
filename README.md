# System Swiped 📸

Swipe your photos like a game. **Left = delete. Right = keep.** Earn points and build your camera!

It’s free. No account. Works without internet.

## 📱 iPhone

### Step 1: Open this link in **Safari**

### 👉 https://mrbrodyturner-cyber.github.io/system-swiped/

(It has to be **Safari**, the blue compass app. Chrome won’t work.)

### Step 2: Put it on your Home Screen

1. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow ⬆️).
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**.

Now open **Swiped** from your Home Screen. 🎉

### Step 3: Swipe!

1. Tap **Choose photos**.
2. Pick your photos. *(Tip: touch one photo, slide sideways, then drag down to pick lots fast. Do about 200 at a time.)*
3. Swipe **left** to delete, **right** to keep.

### Step 4: Delete the ones you swiped left

The first time, the app will ask you to **add a shortcut**. Do it once:

1. Tap **Delete rejects**.
2. Tap **Get the shortcut**.
3. Tap **Add Shortcut**.
4. Go back to Swiped and tap **I added it, delete now**.
5. Tap **Allow**, then confirm.

After that, just tap **Delete rejects** every time. ✅

**Deleting a LOT of photos?** Do this one time:
**Settings → Shortcuts → Advanced → turn on “Allow Deleting Large Amounts of Data.”**

*Your deleted photos go to **Recently Deleted** in the Photos app. You can get them back for 30 days.*

## 💻 Mac

### Step 1: Download it

### 👉 [Download for Mac](https://github.com/mrbrodyturner-cyber/system-swiped/releases/latest/download/System-Swiped-1.0.0-Mac.dmg)

The file is called **System-Swiped-1.0.0-Mac.dmg**.

### Step 2: Install it

1. Open the file you downloaded.
2. Drag **System Swiped** into the **Applications** folder.

### Step 3: Open it the first time

Your Mac will stop it the first time (that’s normal).

1. Try to open **System Swiped**. Click **Done** on the warning.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.

Now it opens normally every time. 🎉

*Needs macOS 15 or newer.*
