# System Swiped

Swipe through your photos like a game: **left to cut, right to keep.** Earn XP, build your dream camera rig, unlock backgrounds and badges. Free, and works offline.

---

## 📱 iPhone app (free, no download needed)

**Open this link in Safari on your iPhone:**

### 👉 https://mrbrodyturner-cyber.github.io/system-swiped/

Then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen → Add**. It gets its own icon, opens full screen, and works offline after that.

- Pick photos from your library (tip: touch a photo, slide sideways, then drag down to select a whole album fast)
- Swipe to cull, then tap **Save keepers** to put your selects back in Photos
- Must be **Safari**. Chrome on iPhone can’t install web apps.

## 💻 Mac desktop app (download)

**File to download:** `System-Swiped-1.0.0-Mac.dmg` (about 180 MB)

### 👉 [Download for Mac](https://github.com/mrbrodyturner-cyber/system-swiped/releases/latest/download/System-Swiped-1.0.0-Mac.dmg)

(or grab it from the [Releases page](https://github.com/mrbrodyturner-cyber/system-swiped/releases))

The desktop version moves files into real **Delete** and **Selected** folders, handles RAW + JPEG pairs, and scores photos with Apple’s on-device vision (focus, exposure, eyes open and more).

**Install:**
1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **System Swiped** into **Applications**.
2. The first time, macOS will block it because it isn’t from the App Store. Double-click it, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
3. After that it opens normally.

**Needs:** macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

---

Everything runs on your device. No account, no internet, no data leaves your phone or computer.
